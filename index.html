Personal AI project

Alma

A personal AI household assistant designed to help a family organize everyday life through WhatsApp.

What is Alma?

Alma is a personal, non-commercial AI household assistant project. It is being developed for private household use and is not a commercial service or registered business.

The goal is to make everyday household coordination simpler by allowing family members to interact with Alma naturally through WhatsApp.

What can Alma do?

  • Keep track of household tasks and open items.
  • Maintain shopping lists.
  • Create and manage reminders.
  • Remember useful household information.
  • Help summarize what is currently saved or still pending.
  • Support private and shared household contexts as the project evolves.

How it works

Alma uses WhatsApp as its primary interface. Messages are processed by the assistant and routed to a controlled memory and task system.

AI is used to help understand natural language, while important actions are validated by deterministic application logic before they are executed.

Privacy and intended use

Alma is intended for private household use. It is not currently offered as a public product or commercial service.

The project is designed around separation between private user information and shared household information, with controlled access to stored data.

Project status

Alma is an actively developed personal software project. The current focus is reliable household memory, reminders, task management, natural-language interaction, and WhatsApp-based communication.
